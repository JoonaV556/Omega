Draw levels in this order for ease of development.
Makes y-sorting of sprites dramatically easier

Y-sorting trick to make y-sorting easier:
	1. When importing tilsheets to a tileset for tilemapping, apply default bottom y-sort to all tiles

Tilemaplayers and their contents:
	1. Background
		- Z index -2 or less
		- Y SORTING DISABLED 
			walls
			floors
			windows
	2. Background overlay
		- Z index higher than layer
		- Y SORTING DISABLED
			Carpets
			floor coverings etc.
			Paintings 
	----> Objects on same layer as player (Player can go behind and in front)
	3. Prop layer 1
		- Y SORTING ENABLED
	4. Prop layer 2 
		- Y SORTING ENABLED
